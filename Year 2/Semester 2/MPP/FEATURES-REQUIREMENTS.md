Website to showcase our comic and get feedback regarding the storyline/chapters.



**All additions run by client before implementation. Source? Trust me bro.**

Should be pretty simple (only email/username/password fields).

**2. LOGIN ------------------------**

Login using username/email and password.

At the bottom: forgot password/sign up.

**ADDED BY ME: Logo visible in the bg**



**3. HOME ------------------------**

**Top:**

Navigation bar with **home**, **chapters**, **character** **index**, and **more** (for other ideas that might pop up later). A profile on the right side of the bar.

**Navbar: on click a new page with the respective title opens. !!!More: on click -> dropdown menu: Sketches, Sillies, About**



**Main:**

Bigger preview of a new page on the left with a description on the right

Under it: A preview of the newest sketch and a description + A preview of the newest silly comic + description

**ADDED BY ME: Logo visible in the bg**

**User clicks any of the previews, their respective pages open.**

**Admins can add/remove any of the descriptions.**



**4. PROFILE ------------------------**

Contains: profile picture on the left, username bold, description.

**User can edit these fields.**



Below: Questions and NEW!!!*Contributions tab*

Questions: user questions + admin answers. **User can add questions.**

*NEW!! Contributions: user interactions with past polls/votes. **User can click on one of their votes to see the whole poll.***
